Sintulus 6 is our strongest tier, rebuilt from the ground up. For research with real computation and review, for code at its hardest points and for work that takes hours rather than minutes.

Sintulus 5 is the tier for tasks where the result has to be right. Sintulus 6 is meant to hold that standard over long stretches: a research question from the dataset to the verified figure, a refactor across a whole project, a task you hand over in the morning and sign off in the evening.

To do that it does not work alone. A plan, several specialised steps and a reviewer that holds the result against the log — visible, traceable, stoppable.

What changes

  1. 01

    Work that runs for hours

    Tasks keep running as their own run even when the window is closed, and check back when they are done or need a decision.

  2. 02

    Research with evidence

    Numbers come from the source and from real Python, never from the model's memory. Every figure carries its provenance.

  3. 03

    Code across the whole project

    Changes across many files with a plan, checkpoints and tests — instead of single suggestions you have to piece together yourself.

  4. 04

    Spatial understanding

    Read and create molecules, structures and scenes in 3D — rotatable in the browser, not a flat picture.

Where it runs

Everywhere you pick a model tier today — no separate access and no new keys.

  • Chat Everyday questions, long threads
  • Code Real projects, real terminal
  • Science Databases and a Python chamber
  • Cowork Finished files, ready to sign off
  • Public API Same tier, same key

Availability and pricing

Like Sintulus 5, Sintulus 6 runs on credits, not on your plan's included allowance. The price follows the model's cost and will be listed in the console at launch.

Until then Sintulus 5 remains the top tier — anything you build with it today can be carried on with Sintulus 6 later.

Questions

Do I need a particular plan?

No, only credits. Your plan sets the other tiers and limits; Sintulus is always billed directly against credits.

What happens to Sintulus 5?

It stays selectable for a transition period after launch. We will announce the period at launch.

Why so late in the year?

Because we only release Sintulus 6 once it passes the checks we learned from Sintulus 5. An early date is worth less to us than a model you can trust with long tasks.
